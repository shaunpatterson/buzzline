AI Overview
UltraBERT is a family of high-performance English transformer encoders developed by DMIS Lab that excels at both text classification and dense retrieval within a single model architecture. Hugging Face +1

Key Features

  • Dual-Task Excellence: Solves the historical trade-off where low masking ratios favor classification and high masking ratios favor retrieval. UltraBERT mixes 5% and 50% masking ratios in every training batch and assigns a decoupled LM head to each ratio. arXiv +1
