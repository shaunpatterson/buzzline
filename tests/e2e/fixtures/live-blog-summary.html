Live updates: flights resume after nationwide ground stop

Here's the latest

• Travel misery: Thousands of travelers face an uncertain weekend at airports across the country because of delays amid yesterday's nationwide hourslong ground stop, forced by a telecommunications outage. Some domestic flights are now resuming but airlines face a battle to clear the backlog. Here's what to do if your flight is canceled or delayed.

• Possible cause: The administration has blamed old air traffic control technology and overloaded routers, pointing to updates to those aging systems. The ground stop did not stem from a threat and was not linked to this week's disruptive cloud outage, the homeland security secretary said.

• Calls for funding: The transportation secretary urged Congress to provide funds for improvements and said it's "going to be slow" to return to normal, adding that it shouldn't affect holiday travel later this month.
