City council approves new transit plan after marathon session

The city council voted seven to two on Tuesday night to approve a ten-year transit plan that will add three bus rapid transit lines.

The vote came after more than six hours of public comment, much of it from residents of the eastern neighbourhoods the new lines will serve.

Supporters said the plan would cut commute times for thousands of workers who currently need two or three transfers to reach the city centre.

Opponents argued that the projected costs were too optimistic and that construction would disrupt small businesses along the main corridors for years.

What happens next

Construction on the first line is expected to begin next spring.

The transit authority will publish a detailed schedule for the remaining two lines before the end of the year, along with revised cost estimates.

Council members asked staff to report back every six months on spending, delays and the effect on local businesses.
